The Frontier

Events

The frontier changes around the player. Scheduled outbreaks, persistent world systems, and special locations create reasons to prepare, travel, and return.

Frontier Events

Western Dead

Infected Outbreaks

At night, an eligible settlement can be selected for an infected outbreak. The town enters a warning period, civilians begin clearing the area, hostile infected build up, and dangerous weather settles over the event zone.

Blackwater is protected from automatic selection because it serves as a new-player starting area. It remains available for controlled testing.

Open Hordes & Events

Blackwater

Auction House

The Blackwater Auction House is a persistent player-and-NPC marketplace built around live auction cycles. Players can browse active lots, bid on listings, consign items for sale, recover unsold goods, claim completed sales, and review auction history.

NPC lots rotate through the house and NPC bidders participate alongside players, allowing auctions to continue as an active world system rather than a static shop menu.

How Events Fit the World

Prepare Before Traveling

Events can temporarily make familiar territory much more dangerous. Carry food, medicine, ammunition, and a reliable escape plan before entering an affected settlement.

Persistent Activities

Not every event is a combat encounter. Systems such as the Auction House give the frontier recurring activity and reasons to revisit established locations.
