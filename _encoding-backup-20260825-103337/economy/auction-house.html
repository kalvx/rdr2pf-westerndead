Frontier Economy

Auction House

A Blackwater player-and-NPC auction economy with rotating lots, market history, bidding, rare firearms, and dynamic price guardrails.

Blackwater

Living Auction Market

The auction scene is centered near -798.90, -1205.30, 43.29 with an auctioneer, seated and standing crowd, clerk, podiums and ambient activity. Players can list eligible goods, set reserve prices, bid on active lots, collect proceeds, and retrieve unsold items.

NPC Economy

Market That Moves Without Players

NPC lots rotate through the market and NPC buyers can bid according to category, price and demand. Recent real sale history gently influences future prices while category guardrails keep values from drifting out of control.

Market Features

What Can Appear

General Goods

Food, ammunition, materials, animal goods, herbs, collector items and jewelry rotate as NPC lots.

Rare Firearms

Occasional weapon lots include Cattleman and Double-Action revolvers, Schofield, Volcanic Pistol, Lancaster, Bolt Action, Rolling Block, Double-Barrel and Pump-Action firearms.

Rare Goods

Gold nuggets, Gold Bars, exceptional jewelry and legendary pelts can appear with low spawn chances and higher price bands.
