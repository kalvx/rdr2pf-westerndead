Player Guide

Getting Started

RDR2PF: Western Dead is a persistent Western survival experience built around making a life on the frontier. There is no single path forward. Survive, earn a living, explore, improve your character and prepare for a world that can become dangerous without warning.

Your First Days on the Frontier

Your character begins with modest means. Food, water, ammunition, transportation and money all matter, and many of the frontier's conveniences must be earned rather than assumed. Learn your surroundings first, establish a source of income and avoid spending everything you have before you are prepared to travel.

Establish Yourself

Get familiar with your starting town, locate useful stores and services, keep food and water available and begin earning cash. Your character, appearance, possessions and progression persist between sessions.

Your Horse

Horses are more than transportation. Stable ownership, horse equipment and your relationship with the animal become an important part of traveling and working across the frontier.

Earn a Living

Hunting, fishing, crafting, mining, lumber work, gathering, trading and Gold Prospecting provide different ways to build wealth. You do not need to follow every profession.

Prepare Before Traveling

The wilderness is not empty. Predators, hostile encounters, hunger, thirst and the Infected can turn a routine journey into a survival problem. Carry what you expect to need before leaving civilization behind.

Unlock the Wayfarer's Posts

Fast travel is not simply handed to a new character. Early frontier progression includes earning access to the Wayfarer's Post network. Until that access is unlocked, your horse and the open road remain your primary means of long-distance travel.

Earn Your Passage

Complete the required frontier challenge and earn permission to use the Wayfarer's Posts. The network becomes a meaningful progression reward rather than an automatic convenience.

Open the Frontier

Once unlocked, Wayfarer's Posts make established destinations much easier to reach and connect distant professions, merchants, events and activities across the map.

Choose Your Frontier Life

Gold Prospecting

Dedicated Gold Panning territories along frontier rivers offer a slower but potentially valuable profession. Prospecting requires the proper equipment and access, and recovered Gold Dust can eventually become part of the wider Gold economy.

Hunting & Wilderness

Animals provide resources and income, but the backcountry also contains dangerous predators. Hunting can take you far from the safety and services of established towns.

Trade & Auction

Stores provide ordinary frontier commerce while the auction economy creates another way for valuable goods to change hands. Wealth can be saved, traded or invested into better equipment and long-term goals.

Companions & Dogs

You do not always have to travel alone. Hired companions and pack dogs can become part of your frontier life, while horses remain central to transportation and exploration.

Western Dead

The frontier is still Red Dead country, but something is wrong with it. Infected outbreaks can overtake settlements and temporarily transform familiar towns into survival zones. Reports and warnings give you a chance to prepare, flee or take part in defending the affected area.

Outbreaks

An ordinary destination may not remain ordinary. When an outbreak reaches a town, expect danger, infected activity and a very different atmosphere until the event burns itself out.

Survive or Fight

You are not required to throw yourself into every outbreak. Enter prepared if you intend to fight. Surviving the frontier is more important than winning every encounter.

A Living Frontier

The world does not exist only around your current character. Other established characters may appear around towns and the wilderness as part of the living population, traveling and going about frontier activities. The intent is for settlements and roads to feel inhabited even during quieter sessions.

Long-Term Progression

Better equipment, valuable horses, Gold, premium weapons and other rewards are intended to take time. The frontier is built around gradual advancement rather than immediately handing a new character everything worth owning.

Take Your Own Route

There is no requirement to rush toward the most expensive weapon or richest profession. Hunt, prospect, explore, trade, travel with companions, survive outbreaks or simply build the kind of frontier character you want to play.

Where to Go Next

For a more detailed first-session walkthrough, continue to the Beginner Guide and Survival Basics. You can also learn about Wayfarer Travel, Gold Prospecting, the Auction House, Pack Dogs, Hired Companions, Horses and the Infected.
