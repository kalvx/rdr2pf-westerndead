Getting Started

Beginner Guide

Practical frontier advice for the things new players usually discover the hard way: supplies, useful resources, money, travel, Gold, and what is worth keeping.

First Things Worth Knowing

Western Dead is easier when you understand which ordinary-looking resources matter. A beginner can lose more time by throwing away useful supplies than by choosing the wrong profession.

Water Matters

Do not leave town assuming water will sort itself out. Replenishable water sources exist around the frontier, and knowing where they are can save a long trip back to town. Keep enough water available before hunting, prospecting or traveling far from services.

Keep Salt & Sugar

Salt and sugar may look like ordinary store goods, but ingredients like these can become useful for cooking and crafting. Do not automatically sell or discard basic supplies just because they are not immediately valuable.

Food Before Distance

Carry something to eat before committing to a long ride. Hunting can provide meat, while stores and cooking offer other options. The farther you travel from a settlement, the more annoying a simple lack of food becomes.

Ammo Is a Resource Too

Do not spend everything on a weapon and leave yourself unable to feed it. Keep enough ammunition for the trip you are actually planning, especially before entering predator country or an active Western Dead area.

Do Not Throw Everything Away

Many frontier items have more value as ingredients, profession supplies or trade goods than their immediate store price suggests.

Check Before Selling

Before getting rid of an unfamiliar item, check whether it belongs to cooking, crafting, hunting, prospecting, trading or another profession. Mundane materials can become important later.

Materials Build Options

Keeping a small reserve of useful materials gives you more choices when recipes, crafting opportunities or profession requirements appear. Selling everything immediately can turn a small future task into another supply run.

Blackwater Auction House

Blackwater is more than another town. The Auction House gives valuable goods another path into the economy and can be useful when ordinary store prices do not make sense for what you are carrying.

Check What You Have

Before assuming an item is worthless, consider whether another player might want it. The Auction House can provide another way to sell useful, uncommon or profession-related goods.

Check Before Buying New

The Auction House can also be worth checking before purchasing certain goods elsewhere. What appears there depends on what players place into the market, so availability can change.

See the Auction House guide for the full system.

Gold Dust Is Not Gold

Gold Prospecting has its own progression loop. Panning in designated river territories produces Gold Dust, not immediately spendable Gold.

Pan in Gold Territory

Gold Prospecting takes place at established panning territories along frontier rivers. Bring the required equipment and make sure you have access before committing to a long prospecting trip.

Take Dust to Van Horn

Recovered Gold Dust becomes useful through the Van Horn assay process. Think of the dust as raw material that still needs to be processed before it becomes part of the wider Gold economy.

See Gold Prospecting for the complete profession guide.

Wayfarer Travel Troubleshooting

If you find a Wayfarer's Post and cannot use it yet, do not immediately assume the post is broken. Access to the travel network must be earned.

Not Unlocked Yet?

Complete the required frontier progression that grants Wayfarer access. Until then, your horse remains the normal way to cross long distances.

Travel Changes the Map

Once unlocked, the network makes distant professions, towns, merchants and events much easier to reach. It is one of the most useful early progression rewards.

See Wayfarer Travel for details.

Wilderness Advice

Predators Are Real Problems

Mountain lions, wolves, coyotes and bears can turn an ordinary wilderness trip into a fight quickly. Do not assume every animal will flee when you approach.

Your Horse Still Matters

Fast travel does not replace having a reliable horse. Your horse remains important for hunting, wilderness work, local travel and reaching places that are nowhere near a Wayfarer's Post.

The Infected Can Change Your Plans

An outbreak can make a familiar town temporarily dangerous. You are allowed to avoid it. Fighting the Infected is a choice, not a mandatory beginner objective.

Leave Prepared

Water, food, ammunition and a plan for getting home matter more once you leave town. Most beginner problems are easier to prevent than to solve halfway across the map.
