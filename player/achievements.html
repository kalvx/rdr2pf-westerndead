Character Deeds

Achievements

Important moments in your character's life can become remembered deeds. Hunt enough game, survive enough infected, work the rivers, cook for the trail or build an outlaw reputation and the frontier may remember what you accomplished.

Lifetime Milestones

How Achievements Work

RDR2PF achievements are called Deeds. They use the lifetime statistics already recorded for your character.

The Deeds system does not maintain a second set of counters. Instead, it reads your existing character statistics and checks whether you have crossed one of the configured milestones.

When a milestone is reached, the deed is remembered permanently for that character and its reward is granted once.

There is no claim button to repeatedly press and no need to restart your progress. The system periodically checks active characters for newly completed deeds.

Recognition

Deed Remembered

When a new milestone is recognized, you receive an on-screen DEED REMEMBERED notification showing the deed name and its reward.

Permanent Record

Rewards Are Given Once

Each deed is saved against the individual character after its reward is successfully granted.

Once remembered, that same character cannot repeatedly receive the reward simply by reconnecting or triggering another achievement check.

Your lifetime statistic continues increasing after a deed is completed. That allows later milestones in the same activity to unlock naturally without resetting your earlier progress.

Existing Characters

Past Progress Still Matters

Because Deeds read your existing lifetime statistics, the system can recognize progress that was recorded before the achievement itself was added.

If a character already exceeds a deed threshold when the system checks their record, that deed can be recognized without making the character perform the activity all over again.

Character History

The Frontier Remembers

Titles describe what your character has become known for. Statistics preserve the numbers behind their history. Deeds mark the moments when that history becomes worth remembering.
