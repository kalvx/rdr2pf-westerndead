Frontier Travel

Wayfarer's Posts

A ten-stop fast-travel network unlocked through ownership and the Valentine corral-fight permit quest.

Requirement 1

Own a Horse

The character must own at least one horse in the stable database.

Requirement 2

Own a Wagon

The character must also own at least one cart/wagon. Wayfarer's Posts are an earned convenience, not a free teleport network.

Requirement 3

Carry the Permit

The wayfarerspermit item must be in your inventory when the post verifies access.

Unlock Guide

Earn the Wayfarer's Permit

  1. Own a horse and wagon. The Valentine stable quest explains that the permit is earned after you have both.
  2. Visit the Valentine stable after dark. Speak with the permit NPC at -353.76, 775.63, 116.12.
  3. Protect the stable. The fight stages five attackers approaching from the west/northwest. The combat start is -367.16, 787.98, 116.16.
  4. Receive the Wayfarer's Permit. Successful completion places the permit in your inventory.
  5. Verify if needed. The NPC menu includes Verify Wayfarer's Permit to refresh travel-post access after reconnects or maintenance.
