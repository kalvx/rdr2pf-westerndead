Profession Guide

Gold Prospecting & Assay

Pan designated rivers for Gold Dust, follow Gold Rush rumors, and refine the haul into Gold Bars at the Van Horn Assay Office.

Frontier Profession

From River Gravel to Gold Bars

Prospecting requires a goldpan, a gold_panning_license, and the character standing in water inside a configured reach. A wash cycle lasts 60 seconds and can repeat for up to 10 cycles before the player confirms again.

Successful washes produce Gold Dust. The current assay economy is 250 Gold Dust = 1 Gold Bar.

Gold Rush Days

Richer Color on One Reach

Periodic Gold Rush days select a prospecting reach and broadcast frontier-style rumors naming the river. During the rush, successful pans at that chosen reach can uncover an extra pocket of fine gold. Infected-town music has priority over prospecting music so the two soundtracks do not play over one another.

Van Horn

Gold Assay Office

Bring Gold Dust to the Van Horn Assay Office at 2936.05, 560.65, 43.93. The office assays up to six bars in one batch. Each bar consumes 250 dust and takes 8 seconds of processing time.

The station includes a scale, ingot mold, pan fire, ore bucket, and money bag to make the office feel like a working frontier assay room.

Known Cosmetic Issue

Panning idle movement

The panning sequence currently uses a crouched inspect scenario. Rockstar's ambient scenario can occasionally add a head scratch or fly-swat movement, causing the attached pan to move with the hand. Weapon holstering has been fixed and the pan attachment has been lowered; the remaining ambient hand movement is tracked as a cosmetic bug.
