What belongs here

This page lists consumable combat ammunition and expendable weapon supplies used by the server. That includes firearm cartridges, arrows, bolas, throwing knives, tomahawks, explosives and similar ammo* inventory records.

Arrowheads and other raw materials are crafting components, not ammunition themselves. They remain documented in the item database and may be used by future or verified crafting recipes.
