Character & Frontier Life

Jobs & Professions

Make your living however the frontier allows. Hunt, fish, prospect for gold, work the land, craft goods, practice medicine, trade, or build a reputation outside the law.

Choose Your Own Path

Life on the Frontier

RDR2PF does not lock every character into a single career. Many professions are open to anyone willing to obtain the right tools, travel to the right place, and put in the work.

Some occupations, such as medical work, may use formal jobs or ranks. Others are activities you can pursue naturally while exploring the world. Your character can hunt one day, prospect the rivers the next, and spend the evening cooking or crafting the materials you gathered.

Wilderness

Hunting

Track wildlife across the frontier, harvest useful animal products, and bring your haul back to civilization. Hunting can provide meat, pelts, antlers, feathers and other materials used throughout the economy.

Gold Country

Gold Prospecting

Take a gold pan into designated river reaches and search the gravel for Gold Dust. Prospectors can follow Gold Rush rumors for richer opportunities and bring their dust to the Van Horn Assay Office.

The current assay rate is 250 Gold Dust for 1 Gold Bar.

Professions Work Together

A Connected Frontier Economy

Most professions are designed to overlap rather than exist in isolation. A hunter can provide meat for cooking and hides for trade. A miner or lumberjack can supply materials for crafting. A farmer can produce ingredients needed by cooks, traders and other players.

Gold Prospecting has its own frontier loop: obtain the required equipment and license, travel to a productive river reach, recover Gold Dust and bring it to Van Horn for assay into Gold Bars.

Character Progression

Your Occupation Does Not Define You

A profession is something your character does; it does not have to be everything your character is. You are free to develop several parts of frontier life as your story grows.

Your repeated actions can also contribute to Living Mastery, allowing the way you actually play to become part of your character's long-term identity.
