Western Dead

Infected Outbreaks

A rotating nighttime town event with warning reports, evacuation, outbreak combat, corpse searching, rewards, and a gradual return to normal.

Western Dead World Event

Reports Before the Fall

Outbreaks begin with ominous reports naming the selected settlement, followed by a warning countdown before the town becomes active. Reports can mention dead telegraph lines, abandoned stagecoaches, screams outside town, or a sheriff asking for deputies.

The automatic event is built around nighttime outbreaks. The infected phase uses outbreak atmosphere and fog, then transitions through burnout and recovery so the town gradually returns to normal rather than snapping back instantly.

Event Flow

Warning → Active → Burnout → Recovery

WarningTelegraph-style report, evacuation behavior, warning banner and countdown.
ActiveInfected spawn inside the selected town, local outbreak music can begin, lawmen may remain to defend the settlement, and infected corpses can be searched.
BurnoutViolence begins to subside while the area remains dangerous.
RecoveryRoads reopen and the world population/weather normalize gradually.

The scheduler prevents an immediate repeat of the same town when alternatives are available.

Corpse Search

Outbreak-spawned infected can be searched during Active and Burnout. Current loot rolls are intentionally sparse: bandages are uncommon, small cash finds are rarer, and most bodies have nothing useful.

Participation Rewards

Rewards scale with participation and survival. Late arrival or brief involvement earns less; stronger participation can pay cash and Gold. Death can reduce the outcome.

Audio Priority

Infected music is town-local rather than server-wide. When the player is inside the active outbreak radius it overrides prospecting music; outside the town, normal activity music is not interrupted.
