The Frontier

Territories

A practical regional guide to where RDR2PF systems change the character of the frontier.

West

New Austin

Armadillo and Tumbleweed are part of the Wayfarer network. Tumbleweed is also central to the gold-prospecting progression because the assay-defense quest is configured there, and both settlements have configured hunting and store services.

Heartlands

Valentine & Dakota country

Valentine is a major service hub with banking, stores, butcher, stable and travel support. Dakota River coordinates are used by the custom gold-panning system, making the surrounding country important for prospecting as well as travel.

South & city

Rhodes, Blackwater and Saint Denis

These settlements carry dense service coverage and are all represented in the travel network. They also have INF1 outbreak definitions, so normally useful town infrastructure can become an active survival space during Western Dead events.

East & north

Van Horn, Annesburg and Wapiti

Van Horn and Annesburg are eastern travel/service anchors and configured outbreak territory. Wapiti is a travel and stable destination with its own shop configuration, giving the northern mountains a functional destination rather than just wilderness.
